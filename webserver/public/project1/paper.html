The raw material for paper is a light-brown bark
This is pulverizes into fine fibers and then soaked in water
.
When the resulting pulp is spread, pressed out on a plank, and dried in the sun it becomes paper -- pure white and so taut that when held between two fingers you can stick a pin in it.
The thrill of making
the first footprint
on an unblemished field of snow.
The instant in which
that first sound is heard
in a still, hushed
pure white paper.
A calligrapher's flickering brush,
a poet's tentative word,
a suitor writes a love letter,
a typographer scrutinizes
the beauty of letters
before applying them to paper.
