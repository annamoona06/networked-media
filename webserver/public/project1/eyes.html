072 Eyes
I once stood completely alone in the middle of a Mongolian plain under a starless jet-black sky. We had three tiny yurts lined up together in a vast land, an expanse so perfectly flat you could see nothing but the horizon. I bedded down by myself, but in the dead of night.
the darkness was full of
in
numerable
bright
eyes
looking at me.
I was so surprised
I was stunned
into silence.
But when I calmed down
and took a better look,
I realized they were the eyes
of grazing sheep
who had gathered in the area
surrounding the yurts.
It was at that moment that I realized eyes give off light.
