I used a Joseon hakuji "water drop" teapot I had on hand. as a reference.
These are small teapots with a full, round bulge at the top.
I considered the tautness of its curved surface.
It gives the feeling that there is some force inside the pot causing it to bulge in this way.
I did not include a handle as I wanted to maintain the pouring method common to such a smooth,
rounded small teapots, which one grasps with a cloth to protect against the heat of the boiled water.
